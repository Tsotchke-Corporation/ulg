There are also two serial dependencies, and both have to move:

- **Batch size.** It is set through the render-ownership policy

### 4.2 What the couplings protect

They protect **exact-generation visibility and buffer lifetime**, not a GPU
data race:
- **One worker, device and queue.** Physics and rendering share them: the live
  app runs the mechanics worker inside the offscreen render worker
  (`ulgOffscreenRender.worker.js:953-958`; bridge message
  `'run-resident-schedule-on-presentation-device'`). So queue order already
  serializes their GPU work.
- **The renderer reads physics buffers in place.**
  - It draws the lane's live state and thermo buffers
    (`ulgOffscreenRender.worker.js:1757-1758`).
  - It reads the product-history and dispersed-medium buffers
    (`ulg-gpu-abi/src/wgsl.js:7893-7906`).
  - Lifetime is guarded by leases and ping-pong storage.

### 4.3 Hazards a naive removal would hit

- **The display could freeze.**
  - The native-surface validation scheduler cancels the in-flight validation
    on every new enqueue (`src/visualization/nativeSurfaceResourceLifecycle.js:470-473`).
  - Publication admission discards a finished frame if physics buffers changed
    during validation (`sphPhaseScene.js:29480-29703`).
  - If physics publishes faster than one validate-and-publish cycle, no frame
    is ever shown.
- **The frame could blank.** The render field writes zeros into the live
  presentation field when the product-history generation moves on
  (`wgsl.js:7893-7906`).
- **The display could starve.** Page draws are skipped while resident GPU work
  is in flight (`sphPhaseScene.js:33161-33216`).
- **Old generations could pile up.** Retiring an SS generation needs its
  render lease released (`schroederSpatialSuccessorSourceFamily.js:1446`). If
  physics outruns frames, generations accumulate unless the scene skips them.
- **Some limits are real and must stay:**
  - The drain every 16 steps bounds Dawn's queue: "unbounded 64-step queue shut
    its Instance down" (worker `:318-325`, verified).
  - A 128-step single-fence batch "could lose the browser WebGPU device"
    (`sphPhaseScenarioPresets.js:280-286`, verified).
  - Long fused submissions delay frames on the shared queue
    (`plan/log.md:44982-44984`).

### 4.4 Pieces that already exist

- **A presentation-owned particle-state copy:** a producer copies live state
  into presentation-owned buffers, and later frames use only those
  (`ulgOffscreenRender.worker.js:4447-4564`).
- **A latest-wins versioned mailbox,** already used by Tier0's post-commit
  continuation (`sphPhaseDemoMount.js:3690-3777`).
- **July's frame stamping and double snapshot:** the renderer refuses frames
  older than the one on screen, and two alternating snapshot buffers fixed the
  tearing (`plan/log.md:38659-38668`, `:40086-40092`).
- **Queue-order commits:** the commit bridge already accepts a commit without
  a GPU fence when queue order is enough
  (`src/runtime/peercomputeResidentCommitBridge.js:146-152`).
- **A non-blocking receipt mode,** "not-attributed"
  (`sphWorkerPresentationQos.js:59-68`).

### 4.5 The G1 decision

- The 09-04 hold exists for G1: "≥54 visibly advancing presentations per real
  wall-clock second across ALL presets" (`plan/log.md:44533-44539`).
- G1's visual acceptance receipts require presented steps == physics steps
  (iron "213/213", `plan/log.md:44020-44028`).
- The cadence dashboard admits a frame only at step == committed + 1
  (`src/visualization/sphPresentationCadence.js:853-859`).
- Decoupling means changing the rule to **"the latest committed state is shown
  at ≥54 Hz"**. That is the user's call (decision 2).

**Step 1 work:**
1. Admit the latest committed epoch.
2. Snapshot at the schedule's terminal submission (extraction is queued, with

3. Make validation latest-wins without cancelling.

## 5. Step 2: contact, moved first, as a correctness test

### 5.1 Overlapping contact responses at the iron–ice interface

1. **The grid multi-field contact.** `contact_fields` runs every substep before
   G2P (`sphMlsMpmGpuStep.js:9548-9690`, verified).
   - `field_contact_pair_policy` makes these co-located field pairs eligible:
     - different condensed materials, such as iron vs ice;
     - the same material as solid vs liquid, such as ice vs meltwater;
     - distinct solid bodies.
   - Gas against condensed is excluded.
   - It applies a perfectly inelastic normal impulse. It knows nothing of the
     pair graph.
2. **The pair-contact graph.** It runs after G2P. Its own comment says that for
   cross-material and distinct-body pairs the reciprocal constraint "belongs to
   the material/domain mechanics-field solve; applying another impulse ...
   would double the response" (`schroederSpatialMechanicalProposalsGpu.js:893-899`,
   verified).
   - Yet the shader computes `barrier_dv = inverse_mass_share * relative_dv`
     for unilateral pairs (`:6722-6726`).
   - Jacobi then applies `pair_scale.y * pair.barrier_dv` (`:7415`), where
     `pair_scale.y` is a coupled `min(position, velocity)` trust scale, not
     zero (`:7237-7256`). All three verified.
3. **The pressure-interface penalty force.** Its elastic term is non-zero at
   rest whenever the gap is under the support radius. It is written into the
   MLS-MPM grid (`src/runtime/sph/sphPressureInterfaceGpuKernel.js:116-117`,
   `:638-712`, `:6423`).
4. **The G2P excluded-volume PBD pair projection,** on by default at relaxation
   0.5 (`src/runtime/sph/sphGpuBuffers.js:73-79`).
5. **A grid wall barrier.** The post-G2P separation pass skips solid–solid
   pairs (`wgsl.js:12614`), so it cannot be the iron–ice fighter.

### 5.2 Precedent for contact fighting physics

The 2026-08-28 comment: the contact stack's "rest-distance projection cancels
the densification a lattice-seeded liquid needs to develop a hydrostatic
gradient" (`sphMlsMpmGpuStep.js:36283-36294`, verified). It held a water cube
rigid at t = 8 s (`sphPhaseScene.js:14374-14421`).

### 5.3 Why the cost is high, and why selection must be on the GPU

- **The interactive budget is below the worst case.** Iron Ice runs 512
  cleanup passes. The batch budget of 1,024 covers "the measured ~890-pass
  iron-ice worst case", and 512 is "below the ~640-serial-pass starvation
  threshold" (`schroederSpatialMechanicalProposalsGpu.js:313-319`, verified).
  So live cleanup can be truncated.
- **Contact fails closed** above 1,024 active particles (`:17138`).
- **Any active law forces contact on:**
  `contactSolverEscalatedForDynamicLaws = !contactSolverRequested && dynamicLawActive`
  (`src/runtime/sph/schroederTargetScheduleAuthority.js:725-741`, verified).
  The rule exists in three drifted copies.
- **Verification forces the full schedule.** Every Jacobi round must count all
  particles, and the cleanup pass count must equal the budget (`:7920-7948`).
  There is no early exit across the 16 Jacobi rounds.
- **Budgets are compiled into WGSL** and sealed into the schedule signature
  (`sphPhaseScene.js:40819-40862`). Host-side budget changes would keep
  changing the signature, so selection has to be decided on the GPU.
- **Mixed-mass chains converge slowly.** Pairwise sweeps converge "only by the
  small light/heavy mass ratio" (`:11312`). That is the likely cause of the
  high pass counts, given the iron/ice mass ratio (inferred).
- **The kept pair set is very wide.** Mixed materials search 33× the largest
  particle diameter (`:4619-4623`).

### 5.4 Tools already in the code

- **The cleanup profile readback,** opt-in `contactCleanupProfileReadback`
  (`sphMlsMpmGpuStep.js:38616-38765`), reachable from the live app.
- **Per-material impulse tracing** (`schroederSpatialMechanicalProposalsGpu.js:14696`,
  `:15519`) and per-pass evidence words.
- **A per-node contact bit in the law queue** (`wgsl.js:14697`). But see
  section 10: the gate that reads it looks indexed by particle.
- **The sodium-water precedent:** 48 cleanup passes × 2 inner rounds sustains
  32 steps/s, against about 7 at 512 (`sphPhaseScenarioPresets.js:305-320`,
  verified).

## 6. Step 3: fusion means rewriting the per-step contract

### 6.1 Blockers

- **One step per call.** The canonical lane throws for more: "Canonical spatial
  authority rejected before execution: one immutable hierarchy generation
  cannot span multiple position epochs" (`sphMlsMpmGpuStep.js:41384-41393`,
  verified).
  - The K-step encoder throws too, because a frozen generation cannot be
    reused (`:14546-14558`).
- **Per-step identity lives in host-written uniforms.**
  - Tick, substep, epochs and lease tokens are written by the host and checked
    by GPU headers.
  - A mismatch silently produces a zero dispatch
    (`ulg-gpu-abi/src/schroederSpatialMechanicsFieldViewWgsl.js:718-721` and
    others).
  - Fusing K steps needs per-step slots or GPU-advanced identity, plus a
    host-visible failure counter.
- **"Submitted" is used as an authority token.** `isExecutionSubmitted` is
  defined in 13 modules (verified by count).
  - The epoch builder submits itself (`src/runtime/sph/schroederSpatialEpochGpu.js:5305`).
  - Consumers require a submitted producer. That forces a `queue.submit`
    inside every step, although queue order alone would satisfy the data
    dependency.
- **No stage runner takes the caller's command encoder.** Side laws run as
  sequential awaited calls with CPU hooks between them
  (`src/runtime/sph/sphMlsMpmPostMechanicsClosure.js:2309-3191`).
- **Arenas cap K.** Epoch, thermal and reaction arenas default to 2–3, with a
  maximum of 8.
  - The thermal arena's release timing is load-bearing.
  - Two fence-coalescing attempts were reverted. Merging fences broke every
    ss=1 run with "Thermal proposal arena 0 is still leased by generation 1"
    (`plan/done/ss-spatial-authority-refactor-merge-readiness.md:1297-1340`,
    verified: "Do not try a third without reading this").
  - Arena leases must become GPU-side or ring-based before fusion. The fence
    count itself is not the cost.
- **Reaction is never quiescent while thermal runs.** The activation watch
  marks every particle triggered whenever thermal or phase is on.
- **Reaction-product pressure needs a hot-path readback today.** A GPU
  finalizer exists, but nothing calls it.

| PeerCompute defers read/write-conflicting stages, but never rejects, and runs only behind `?residentStageWorkers=1` | `peercompute/src/peercompute/computeManager/GpuResidentLaneManager.js:178-288` |

Others:

**Extend one of these so it gates execution and models `accumulates`, rather
than adding a ninth.**

### 6.3 Quick wins to take first

All of these fit the binding rules: no law is removed.
- **Stages with no consumer.**
  - The law queue and four phase-volume stages run every step, and their
    results are never applied. That is about 5 of the 32 submits per step.
  - The bulk-water preset already turns them off as "nothing but waste": the
    law-queue expander "measured 101.8 ms/step at 55k carriers", and migration
    9.5 ms/step (`sphPhaseScenarioPresets.js:514-522`, verified). Iron Ice
    inherits them on.
  - The fix is generic consumer-gated activation, not a per-preset flag.
- **Thermal's all-pairs search on ss=1.** Directory-v2 always selects the
  ActiveSource mode, which visits every active source per source. That is
  about 46M pair checks per pass at 6,784 rows
  (`src/runtime/sph/schroederSpatialThermalProposalsGpu.js:7120-7181`,
  `:10546-10620`). A cell-based walk exists (`:7183-7487`).
- **The redundant S9-C sidecar.** Both consumers recompute its rows.
- **Indirect dispatches.** Each costs about 0.25 ms of CPU in the GPU process.
  Direct ceilings took 20 → 37 steps/s (`plan/log.md:43349-43352`). At 54 per
  step, that is about 13 ms (inferred).
- **Not a lever: per-step buffer creation.** It measured at 1% of batch time
  (createBuffer 0.3%; `plan/done/ss-spatial-authority-refactor-merge-readiness.md:1345-1400`,
  verified).

**Step 3 order:**
1. Attribute the 32 submits per step by stage, unprofiled (Step 0).
2. Take the quick wins.
3. Move epoch identity onto the GPU, and split encode from commit.
4. Add a shared-encoder runner contract.
5. Make the descriptor gate.
6. Move the pressure-interface solver from JS onto the GPU.

    `ulg-gpu-abi/src/sphReactionEnergyWgsl.js:95-103`, verified).
  - Consumption below one f32 ulp of carrier mass is rejected.
  - One bad row empties the whole gas union.
- **Humid-air Iron Ice fails at step 1** on condensable caloric rows
  (`src/runtime/sph/sphReactionGpuKernel.js:323`, `:334`; consistent with
  `46971c0`). If "every law on" includes humid air, Step 4 or an f32
  condensable-reaction path is a prerequisite.
- **Ledgers must cover mass outside particle mass:**
  - the f32 rescale of product mass;
  - unplaced product mass fed to P2G as a sidecar;
  - gas moles, which exist only in a readback.
- **Existing integer pieces to unify with, not duplicate:**
  - the unsigned exact-mass inventory, live in ~15 modules;
  - vapor exchange's 10-limb integer accumulator;
  - P2G's 16.16 fixed-point atomics on the V1 route.
- **Energy checks that can never fail.** Their replacement is the ledgers' job:
  - pressure-interface "conservation" adds each force to its own negation;
  - the reflux ledger's operator-split bound is a triangle inequality;
  - one phase-volume transport energy check can never fail
    (`ulg-gpu-abi/src/schroederSpatialPhaseVolumeTransportWgsl.js:963-968`).

## 8. The gate

**≥1,000 steps/s with every law on is not reachable by Steps 1–4 as written.**
- **The budget is about 1 ms per step,** for all GPU work plus rendering on one
  shared queue.
- **Today's step is about 60 ms of GPU time,** in the profiled variant.
- **Fusion does not remove most per-step GPU work:**
  - two radix sorts per epoch rebuild, every step (the exactness contract
    requires same-step positions);
  - reaction placement at 150–250 capacity-sized dispatches even when nothing
    reacts (inferred);
  - contact;
  - thermal's all-pairs search;
  - about 54 indirect dispatches.
- **The hold alone caps the rate below the gate.** 16-step batches that each
  wait at least one 60 Hz frame give 960 steps/s at zero GPU cost (inferred).
- **Tier0's 1,100–1,260 steps/s is not like-for-like:**
  - it runs no contact graph and no field contact;
  - it runs one row per particle, where Iron Ice runs 4 (1,696 particles →
    6,784 rows);
  - it runs older shaders.
- **"Every law on" is ill-defined today:**
  - vapor exchange is not wired;
  - the far-field "laws" (radiation, plasma, gas summary) are scaled-gravity
    placeholders (`wgsl.js:17194-17209`);
  - the two-level route runs only on the cesium-fluorine preset
    (`sphPhaseScenarioPresets.js:387`).

**Proposal:** staged gates, each measured after its step with the Step 0
method. Rate counts reactions and transfers that were actually committed, not
laws that are switched on. Candidate numbers from the 10-27 data, to be
re-based after Step 0:
- **After Step 1:** the idle half of wall time is gone, so about 13 steps/s.
- **After Step 2:** approach the route's ~35 steps/s ceiling (the rate with a
  free contact solver).
- **After Step 3:** a per-step GPU budget that the user sets.

**dt is a lever.**
- Stiffness is already scaled to the fixed carrier dt:
  `phaseSoundSpeedScaleFor` runs each phase as stiff as the CFL limit allows
  (`src/runtime/sph/sphMechanicsMaterialTable.js:106-118`, verified).
- Sodium-water already runs at 1 ms (`sphPhaseScenarioPresets.js:287`,
  verified).
- Doubling dt halves the steps/s needed for real time.

## 10. Other findings

Grouped by kind. Unless marked verified, these are slice-reader reports.

**Possible bugs (need a test)**
- **Phantom product mass.** The apply kernel can skip a stale pair, but for
  non-physical reactions `physical_reaction_pair_applied` always returns true.
  So the event kernel may book unplaced product mass with no reactants
  consumed, and the summary ledger books the same events (`wgsl.js:2890`,
  `:6892-7008`; inferred).
- **The law-queue contact gate looks indexed by particle.** The queue is
  ordered by active node (`wgsl.js:11594-11621` vs `:14684-14692`; inferred,
  strong).
- **Moved-list overflow.** Overflow of the 512-slot moved list cuts off that
  pass's state copy, so corrections are probably lost at impacts
  (`schroederSpatialMechanicalProposalsGpu.js:17796-17825`).
- **Three-body blocks may collide.** A heavy particle can be claimed by two
  three-body blocks in one pass, with the last write winning (`:12464-12513`,
  `:17580-17600`).
- **Latent ReferenceErrors:**
  - `refreshMlsMpmGridUpdate` references an out-of-scope `executionGeneration`
    (`sphPhaseScene.js:36274`);
  - a default parameter at `sphPhaseScene.js:10113`.
- **A missing thermal-environment wall face defaults to a 0 K reservoir**
  (`src/runtime/thermalEnvironmentAuthority.js:170-179`).
- **Wave speed has two definitions.**
  - The material table uses the p-wave speed √((K+4/3 G)/ρ).
  - `sphPhaseDemo.js:1175-1190` uses the bulk-only √(K/ρ), which feeds
    `maxRealSoundSpeed`.
- **P2G 16.16 fixed point on the V1 route** (bulk-water, water-realtime):
  contributions below about 7.6e-6 kg·m/s round to zero
  (`wgsl.js:10104`, verified for the V1 route). This does not apply to Iron
  Ice's field route (`sphMlsMpmGpuStep.js:5878-5893`, verified).

**Silent failure modes**
- **Rollbacks are invisible.** A GPU authority rollback silently becomes a
  mechanics no-op (`sphMlsMpmGpuStep.js:13579-13582`, verified).
- **One bad row can cancel a whole level.** A single bad particle row cancels
  its level's dispatch with no error (`schroederSpatialMechanicsFieldViewWgsl.js:718-721`).
  Transport and surface-stress failures zero the indirect args.
- **GPU errors go unseen.** `src/` and `ulg-gpu-abi/src` have no
  `uncapturederror` listener (verified by grep).
- **Three executors report `'queue-work-completed'` without waiting**
  (`peercomputeBrowserResidentHost.js:2105`, `:2347`, `:2592`).
- **A timeout leaves an orphan.** The 30 s stage timeout never cancels the
  schedule, so the orphan keeps advancing lane state without a commit
  (`ulgOffscreenRender.worker.js:696-706`).

**Memory growth at high step rates**
- `device.lost` handlers are attached per step or per call in many modules,
  and never removed.
- PeerCompute's `setHotBuffer` is a bare `Map.set` that never frees
  (`peercompute/src/peercompute/stateManager/DataState.js:95`). Presentation
  keys it by step and time.
- Some caches keyed by particle count or byte size are never pruned.
- Live render surface-summary kernels rebuild shader modules and pipelines on
  every call.

**Fragile code generation**
- **The live thermal shader is text-patched.** It is the ABI kernel plus 12
  exact-text replacements, with tab-indented anchors, so reformatting breaks
  thermal at load (`wgsl.js:1270-1511`).
- **V2 shaders are V1 plus string patches.** Several patches no-op silently on
  drift. This is the seam behind the `6bac50e` rollback bug.
- **Import-time regexes can stop the app loading.** Regexes that run at import
  throw on a miss, so an ABI edit can stop the worker from loading
  (`exactPlacementQuantityWgsl`, `exactReactionSourceGpu`).
- **The reaction-extent law exists in seven copies:**
  - six byte-identical copies of `interface_flux_extent_cap_mol` at
    `wgsl.js:2174`, `5680`, `6344`, `6775`, `7202` and `7499`;
  - a seventh made by regex.
  - The copies exist because downstream kernels recompute the extent instead
    of reading the apply kernel's result. Capping only two of them once made
    1,172 kg of NaOH from 127 kg of Na (`plan/log.md:42064-42067`).

**Size and hygiene**
- **Monolith functions:**
  - `runSchroederSameLevelMechanicsWebGpuImpl`, about 9,190 lines
    (`schroederHierarchyGpu.js:17543-26730`);
  - `createSphPhaseScene`, about a 40,900-line closure;
  - the worker schedule function, about 5,000 lines;
  - `refreshMlsMpmResidentSteps`, about 4,485 lines.
- **A tracked copy of the worker** sits at
  `src/services/ulgMechanicsResidentStage.worker.js.orig` (verified). It
  duplicates the worker's symbols in ICC.
- **A raw NUL byte** at `src/runtime/sphPhaseViewStateTransport.js:98`
  (verified) hides the file from ICC and plain grep. Use `grep -a`.
- **Test-only code ships in production modules:** CPU oracles and mirrors,
  about 1,000 lines in the contact module alone.
