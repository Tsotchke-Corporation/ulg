### Open G2 design decisions

Full scope fixes the direction of the first decision. The second still needs
a choice. Neither is implemented.

1. **Mass precision.** Two f32 endpoints cannot conserve exactly a transfer
   between masses many binades apart. For example, `mg=1.5` and
   `ml=2^-10+2^-33` hold an odd `2^-33` bit. The current writer therefore
   rejects proposals unless old and new masses stay within a factor of 2.
   - The full-scope path is a fully inventoried low part, or higher
     precision, carried through every consumer: EOS/moles/volume,
     P2G/G2P, reaction, phase, thermal, optics, transport.
   - A bounded-error f32 model would narrow G2, so ask the user before
     choosing it.
   - Do not relax the writer gates. Do not count unexecuted flux as
     transferred mass: a delayed-flux accumulator alone cannot cross every
     binade.
2. **Interface and transport closure domain.**
   - Room air (mean free path about 0.07 µm) implies a locally thermalized
     continuum/diffusion closure for the steam presets, with a
     transition-regime correction for nuclei-sized particles.
   - Species-resolved ballistic (large Knudsen number) exchange applies to
     the vacuum benchmarks.
   - The coefficients (accommodation, diffusivity, conductivity) are
     currently uncalibrated inputs. Document sources for them.
   - Research leads are listed in
     `~/.cache/icc/repos/ulg/g2-stage-inputs-20260914-thwXoQ/integration-audit.md`.
