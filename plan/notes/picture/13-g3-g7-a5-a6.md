## A5 solids beyond SOL-1 (SOL-2/3 contact and coupling, SOL-4 deformation, SOL-5 topology, SOL-6, more SS levels)
- Delivered: SOL-3 heat (`schroederSolidFluidThermalGpu.js`) and contact (`schroederSolidFluidContactGpu.js`, frictionless) on the f32 route; SOL-2 body-body contact (2026-11-15); the rigid-shape presenter fallback; the presentation stall fix; N-level chain Phase A (see digest L).
- Live check 2026-11-14 on the Iron Ice box with a rigid ice block: iron melts it from above (20 of 256 members released by 2.0 s).
- Plume census on rigid ice at 6 s simulated (`sdt=0.001`, ~5.5 steps/s then): Fe solid 9.5 g, Fe liquid 23.6 g as a pool on the floor (y <= 9 mm), meltwater 5.2 g at max 293.8 K, H2O gas 0. The iron-water interface carried ~13 W.
- Why no steam there: coarse 0.115 g meltwater parcels heat as a bulk (the real contact layer at ~1,650 K film-boils at once); and "the molten iron runs off the rigid ice onto the floor instead of staying in its crater".
- NOT started: SOL-4 (deformation), SOL-5 (topology: melt/fracture changing a body's shape), SOL-6, chain phases B-E.
- What G2 is waiting on from A5 (Hermes's reading, to confirm with the user): something that keeps molten iron in contact with the ice (crater geometry, i.e. SOL-5-type topology change, or friction; SOL-3 contact is frictionless), so that iron-cools passes (needs 10 K in 4 s; measured 8 K) and steam yield is no longer ~0.16-0.19 mg.
